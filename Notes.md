### Main plot for the game

- define that the protagonist is a loser who can't speak up for himself
- one day he meets a girl he's never seen before
- childhood friend-ish scene where she takes him to the school nurse after he gets badly injured from … what?
- a soccer game?
- an accident on the stairs?
- they go on a date and start a relationship
- "It's been like one date, and he's already calling her his girlfriend"
- protag tries to improve himself for her and change his life
- ends up lying about himself to try and make himself sound better than he actually is
- girl realizes he's lying and cuts it off
- he tries to improve himself for the better, but it's already too late
- add some moral about not lying about yourself just to appear better to others


### Two (three?) main love interests

#### TAMERA - the bitchy loner
 - is isolated like Kunabo, but doesn't seem to be bothered by it
 - treats people exactly how she thinks they should be treated
 - doesn't give a shit
- has a lot of friends outside of school
- spends most of her time on academics and hobbies, and doesn't bother dealing with people

#### NARU - childhood friend
- known Kunabo since elementary school, but they didn't talk much until last year
- shipped together with kunabo by their friend groups but hated it
- knows how to crack a good joke
- she doesn't seem to be interested in relationships
- that doesn't mean kunabo isn't

> sakura-death was originally planned to have 3 main love interests you could pick from, but Kina was completely cut out. Naru was going to have her own dedicated route related to Kubano joining debate club to impress her. (might be in a future update? maybe)

#### KINA - meek butterfly
- always surrounded by people but somehow still shy
- has some sort of codependent thing going on with her friends
- always tries to get on people's good side, which leads to a lot of misunderstandings
- Is her shyness some sort of mask?

### misc facts:

- kunabo comes from the Japanese phrase 'kodokuna katsubo,' translating to 'lonely desire'
- tamera comes from the Japanese translation for 'hesitate,' which is 'tamerau'

### goals for the game:

- present characters, especially love interests, like real people with fluctuating personalities and varying hobbies/interests
- depict depression and social anxiety in a realistic and relatable way
- stick to a single visual style for the entire game
- make sure plot points are consistent and up-to-date
- logically, maybe about 4-7 main game endings with 2-3 joke endings? (may change depending on story flow)
