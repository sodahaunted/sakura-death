<h2>Game Premise</h2>

You play as a boy named Kunabo who has suffered from social anxiety and depression his whole life. He struggles with going to school, his grades are at an all-time low, and he has no hobbies other than watching livestreams to pass the time. Despite all of this, his biggest goal in life has been to make a friend with similar circumstances to his to have someone to relate to about his struggles. All of his luck seems to strike out when he becomes partners with the meanest girl in school for a group project. Will he be able to befriend the plucky mean girl, or give up his dream for good?

<br>

<h2>Project Info</h2>

- Built in pure Google Docs, with story choices redirecting to separate pages using hyperlinked text
- Indie horror-game-inspired graphics and art style
- About ~20 minutes of playtime for 100% completion
- 15+ choices that directly influence the plot
