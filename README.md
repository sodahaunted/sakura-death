<h2>Story Premise</h2>

You play as a boy named Kunabo who has suffered from social anxiety and depression his whole life. He struggles with going to school, his grades are at an all-time low, and he has no hobbies other than watching livestreams to pass the time. Despite all of this, his biggest goal in life has been to make a friend with similar circumstances, someone he can relate to about his struggles. All his luck seems to run out when he partners with the meanest girl in school for a group project. Will he be able to befriend the plucky mean girl, or give up his dream for good?

<br>

<h2>Project Info</h2>

- Built in pure Google Docs, with story choices redirecting to separate pages using hyperlinked text
- Indie horror-game-inspired graphics and art style
- About ~20 minutes of playtime for 100% completion
- 15+ choices that directly influence the plot
